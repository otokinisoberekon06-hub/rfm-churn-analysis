Insight Brief · Findings

3 Key Findings

FINDING 01

Churn Is Costing More Than It Appears

58.1% of customers have not purchased in 64+ days. These churned customers represent £3,783,910 — 25.7% of all lifetime revenue ever generated. For every £4 earned, £1 came from a customer who has since stopped returning.

FINDING 02

Strong Repeat Rate, Narrow Revenue Base

A 72.4% repeat purchase rate exceeds the 35% industry benchmark — but 41.9% of customers carry 74.3% of revenue. The loyalty foundation is strong; the concentration creates fragility.

FINDING 03

Revenue Is Dangerously Concentrated

21.1% of products generate 80% of all revenue. The top product alone contributes £234,185. A supply disruption on any top-20 SKU has an immediate, measurable revenue impact.

Insight Brief · Action

3 Specific Recommendations

01

Launch a 30-Day Win-Back Campaign for At Risk Customers

The At Risk segment (245 customers, £372,898 in historical revenue) has a proven purchase history. Target those with M_Score ≥ 3. Reactivation cost is a fraction of new customer acquisition — and these customers already know the brand.

02

Protect the Champions Segment Above All Else

Champions generate £10,028,932 — 68.1% of total revenue. No acquisition campaign can quickly replace a drop in Champions activity. Exclusive access, loyalty recognition, and a dedicated communication cadence are non-negotiable investments.

03

Reduce Dependency on Revenue-Concentrated SKUs

Inventory protection and supplier redundancy on the top 20 revenue-generating SKUs is a risk management priority. Introduce adjacent products into high-revenue categories to distribute concentration without disrupting what is working.
